# Trustworthy Calculations Service Reframe — Revision Outline

This is an outline-stage proposal only. It does not authorize changes to production collateral, assets, narration, generated output, deployment, or the live URL.

Claude Opus and Codex reached `CONSENSUS VERDICT: PROCEED` on 2026-07-28 after two bounded review rounds. The consensus requires explicit provenance for the worked calculation, a strict split between demonstrated artifact behavior and proposed calculation controls, review-required AI recovery, narrow illustrative transfer domains, and a bounded calculation-contract first step.

## Concept and purpose

- **One-sentence concept:** Demonstrate how Mike turns messy source data and business definitions into calculations that are explicit, reproducible, validated, and easy to review, using a self-built financial-metrics workflow as one concrete example.
- **Revision purpose:** Replace the specific SEC-pipeline product pitch with a transferable calculation-design story while preserving the existing public-safe evidence as supporting proof.
- **Positioning boundary:** This is not a packaged financial-data product, investment analysis, or a claim that AI should perform arithmetic. The financial workflow is an example of a broader method for trustworthy calculations.

## Audience and viewer job

- **Audience:** Teams that depend on spreadsheets, source documents, APIs, and recurring calculations for operational or financial decisions.
- **Viewer job-to-be-done:** Decide whether Mike can translate a business calculation into a maintainable workflow with defined inputs, transparent formulas, validation checks, exception handling, and reviewable outputs.
- **Desired action / CTA:** Share one recurring calculation or model that is still assembled or checked by hand.
